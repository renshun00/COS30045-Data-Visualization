Our Story

About PowerSense

We believe every Australian household deserves clear, honest information about energy consumption — without wading through jargon or sales pitches.

Our Mission

Making energy visible

PowerSense was founded on a simple observation: most people have no idea how much electricity their appliances actually use. They receive a quarterly bill, feel the shock, and have no way to understand why it's so high or what to do about it.

We set out to change that. By translating raw wattage numbers into real dollar figures and clear comparisons, we help households and small businesses take control of one of their largest recurring expenses.

Our data is sourced from publicly available Australian government datasets, manufacturer specifications, and the Equipment Energy Efficiency (E3) programme — then presented in language that doesn't require an engineering degree to understand.

What We Stand For

Our values

🔎
Transparency

We show our sources, explain our methodology, and never hide caveats in footnotes. Energy data should be trustworthy, not marketing material.

🌱
Sustainability

Lower energy use is better for household budgets and for the planet. We include carbon estimates alongside dollar figures because both matter.

🤝
Accessibility

Our tools are free, require no registration, and are designed to work on any device. Clear language is non-negotiable — if it's not understandable, it's not useful.

📊
Data Integrity

We only publish figures we can cite. When data is estimated or simplified, we say so. Accuracy earns trust; trust is everything.

Methodology

Where our data comes from

🏛️

E3 Programme

The Equipment Energy Efficiency programme is a joint Australian and New Zealand government initiative. It provides mandatory energy performance requirements and labelling standards for appliances.

🔬

DCCEEW

The Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water publishes annual National Greenhouse Accounts, including the emission factors we use for carbon calculations.

📋

Manufacturer Specifications

Wattage and annual energy consumption figures are sourced from manufacturer datasheets and product listing pages, cross-checked against energy label data where available.
